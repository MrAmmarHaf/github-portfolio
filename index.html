About Me

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Game Development at Lamar University, with an expected graduation date of Summer 2027. Although my degree focuses on game development, many of my courses cover core computer science subjects, including algorithms, computer architecture, computer networks, computer theory, and much more. I'm interested in using that foundation to build software that is both technically reliable and enjoyable to use.

My interest in game development started with Unity, where I experimented with different projects and learned the basics of how games are developed. I actually started learning game development before AI coding tools became widely popular, so a lot of my early experience involved figuring things out through research, experimentation, and trial and error. As I became more serious about game development, I decided to move to Unreal Engine 5 and focus more on C++. Since then, I've worked on different gameplay systems, including player movement, combat mechanics, animation, and artificial intelligence. One of my current projects is a third-person combat game where I've been applying what I've learned. I enjoy both the technical and creative sides of game development, especially solving problems and figuring out how different systems work together.

Outside of gameplay programming, I enjoy learning about software engineering, development tools, and how artificial intelligence can help programmers work more effectively. I use Visual Studio and Git to organize and debug my projects, and I continue to build my skills through coursework and personal development.

My goal is to begin my career in the video game industry as a gameplay programmer or in another junior programming role where I can learn from experienced developers and contribute to real projects. I'm open to internships and entry-level opportunities, including roles that would require relocating. This portfolio is a place to share what I've built, explain the technical decisions behind my projects, and show how my skills are developing over time.
